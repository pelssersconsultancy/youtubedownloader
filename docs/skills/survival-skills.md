# Self-Reliance: A Complete Skills List with YouTube Channels

Prepared on November 18, 2026

**How to read this list:** ✓ = the channel appeared in “best channels” lists I looked up. \* = the channel is from my own knowledge and was not checked during this session. Channels change: check that they are still active and watch a few videos before investing a lot of time. I cannot read actual reviews, so “good” here means frequently mentioned in recommendations.

**Safety:** mistakes in canning, fermentation, first aid, welding, and electrical work can be dangerous (botulism, burns, electrocution). For food safety, use tested recipes and treat YouTube as a supplement to books and manuals.

## 10. Offline storage and books

- Download your best videos with yt-dlp and store them on an external drive or USB stick, since YouTube is not guaranteed to remain available.
- Print important guides and keep a few physical books, such as _The Encyclopedia of Country Living_ (Carla Emery) and _Where There Is No Doctor_.
- Practice skills now, not just in a crisis: doing something yourself once is worth more than watching ten videos.
